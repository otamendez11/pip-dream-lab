📖 THE STORY OF PIP (click the chapters!)

Pip was born on the quietest night of the year, when the moon forgot to shine and the stars held their breath. The nurses say his first word was a yawn and his first toy was a dreamcatcher. Nobody knows why his face is a little skull, the Sandman claims it helps babies take him seriously. Pip does not mind. He was employee of the month before he could walk, mostly because nobody else applied for the night shift.

The Midnight Nursery floats somewhere between 11:59 PM and the first birdsong. Its cribs are clouds, its nightlights are bottled fireflies, and its lullabies are composed fresh nightly. Pip is the Junior Dream Engineer: he tunes the Lullaby Engine, untangles nightmare knots, and taste-tests every dream before delivery. His desk is a crescent moon. His stapler is a shooting star. His performance reviews are written in lullaby form.

Last Tuesday the Dream Reservoir ran dry, three million sleepers, zero dreams left. While the senior engineers panicked, Pip did what babies do best: he napped. In his nap he dreamed a dream about dreaming, bottled it, and poured it back into the reservoir. By dawn every sleeper got an extra dream, free of charge. Management promoted him on the spot. His new title is still Junior. He prefers it that way.
